02

Arnauts

After MVTHRSYS. A ship of hull and corridor tiles, crew on A*. Originally libGDX/Java; this is my Phaser port, playable in the browser.

  • Click empty cells to place corridor
  • L drops a ladder · Q / E rotate the ship
  • Arrows pan
Open live demo github.com/adavidev/arnauts

03

Walkmode

Click start, then end. A walkability grid from Terrarium elevation and OSM water; A* with an uphill penalty goes around the hill instead of over it.

  • React 19 · Vite · MapLibre
  • Client-side DEM + Overpass
  • No backend
Open live demo github.com/adavidev/walkmode
