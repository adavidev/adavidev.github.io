Walkmode · contour, don’t climb

01

Walkmode

Click start, then end. It builds a walkability grid from Terrarium elevation tiles and OSM water, then A* with an uphill penalty finds a path that goes around the hill instead of over it.

  • React 19 · Vite · MapLibre
  • Client-side DEM + Overpass
  • No backend
Open live demo github.com/adavidev/walkmode

02

Arnauts

A ship made of hull and corridor tiles. Crew pathfinds with A*. Originally libGDX/Java; this is the Phaser port, running in the browser.

  • Click empty cells to place corridor
  • L drops a ladder · Q / E rotate the ship
  • Arrows pan
Open live demo github.com/adavidev/arnauts
